There’s a specific kind of exhaustion that settles into your bones when you’ve been living in a boarding house for too long. Between the endless cycle of school deadlines, the constantly surviving eating processed food, and the hardship of trying to find a decent night’s sleep in an uncomfortable and unfamiliar bed, I felt like my batteries was coming to it's end.I finally made it back to the province.
My first day home was not just a break; it was a total reset. For the first after 2 weeks, I didn't have to worry about anything. I actually slept deeply knowing I was finally where I belong. Hours went by and i get to spend almost all of my time playing with my cats and dogs. We even managed to go for a night jog, which was the perfect way to have some fresh air away from the polluted environment and cramped corners of my boarding house life. And then, there’s this food.
You don't realize how much you miss a real meal cooked by your mom until you’ve spent weeks living on canned or processed goods. My mom has been cooking all the things I’ve been wanting and craving. I would constantly call her before saying "Mom, di masarap kinain ko" and "Lutoan mo ako ng ano mom pag-uwi ko" My first day was basically my way of taking care of myself. Being back home has been the breath of fresh air I didn't know I needed. I’m finally feeling more energized and ready to go back to school knowing i'll be back again to my province.
