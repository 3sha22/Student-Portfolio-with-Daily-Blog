Daily Blogyy

Day 1

Today, the school announced three days of asynchronous classes, and I felt excited because going home gives me comfort and rest. When I arrived, I saw the kittens outside — they were so cute. I cleaned the house and spent some time scrolling on my phone.I thought about biking around town, but my bike was broken, which made me feel bored. I made a sandwich for merienda and considered sewing, but instead I relaxed and thought about design ideas for my web system project.

Day 2

I woke up to a brownout affecting the whole barangay. I thought it would last only a few hours, but it continued all day. With my phone battery low, I felt very bored. I worked on some embroidery and added a doodle design to a piece I made last year. Later, I played with my cat and craved palabok, so we bought some from Tabaco. In the evening, we went to the sea near our home and swam for a short time before heading back when it got dark.

Day 3

There was still no electricity when I woke up, so I went to my tita’s house to continue my project. I made good progress and enjoyed designing, even though it was sometimes frustrating.After working most of the day, I rested, played with my cat, and cooked something for myself. I washed the dishes, took a shower, and lay down for a nap that turned into a deep sleep.
